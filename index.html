Merry Christmas

A little gift, sent through the snow

穿过雪夜，送给你的小礼物

Christmas Eve, 2026

Dear your name,

写下你的名字，这封信就是你的了

Merry Christmas. I hope this little box found you on a night that smells of pine and candle smoke — the kind of night it was made for.

I couldn't wrap the things I actually wanted to give you, so I put them in the fireflies instead: luck that arrives quietly, beauty you can look at in the mirror without flinching, studies that finally make sense the night before, health that holds all year, friends who answer when you call, a love that is gentle with you, work that feels like yours, and a family that always keeps a light on.

And an apple — because a well-wished apple is supposed to keep you safe until next Christmas. Ours came a little late, is a little sweet, and is entirely yours.

Thank you for being the friend who makes an ordinary Tuesday worth remembering. I hope this year is generous with you; and if it isn't, I'll be here with a smaller box and the same wish.

With all my love, Maren

亲爱的朋友：

圣诞快乐。希望这只小盒子抵达你的时候，窗外正有雪，屋里正有光——这样的夜晚，它才算是送到了。

真正想给你的东西没法包起来，所以我把它放进了那些萤火虫里：安静的运气，镜子里不必躲闪的好看，考试前夜突然通透了然的学业，整整一年都不出岔子的健康，随时接得住你的朋友，温柔待你的爱情，像自己的事业，以及永远为你留着一盏灯的家人。

还有一颗苹果。据说被好好祝福过的苹果，能护你平安到下个圣诞。我们的这颗来得晚了些，甜了一点，全部归你。

谢谢你，是那个把普通星期二过得值得记住的朋友。愿这一年对你慷慨；若它偶尔小气，我还在，带着一只更小的盒子和同样的祝福。

永远爱你的 Maren

P.S. The fireflies are yours now. Keep them somewhere warm. 又及：萤火虫归你了，记得放在暖和的地方。
